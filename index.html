Who We Are

About SANITRO

Building resilient urban infrastructure with smart technology and dignified human care.

SANITRO is an advanced B2G SaaS and urban cleantech platform engineered to revolutionize emergency sewage response and localized flood relief. We bridge the critical gap between affected citizens, frontline sanitation partners, and municipal administration using hyper-local dispatch algorithms and live analytics.

⚡

Rapid Disaster Response

Connecting citizens directly with rapid-response sanitation units within minutes during sewer overflows and rainwater blockages with live SLA tracking.

🛡️

Worker Dignity & Safety

Empowering informal sanitation workers with fair gig income, digital payouts, and mandatory Safety Kit verification for hygienic and safe work conditions.

📊

B2G Governance Intelligence

Providing smart city officers and municipal authorities with high-precision heatmaps, real-time dispatch queues, and predictive monsoon analytics.

🎯 Our Mission

To reduce municipal sanitation response times to under 15 minutes while restoring dignity to frontline environmental workers.

👁️ Our Vision

Zero urban sewage blockages and flood disasters through AI-assisted municipal scheduling and real-time community engagement.
